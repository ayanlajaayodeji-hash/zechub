## Mission Statement

Zcash Labs is an independent organization dedicated to bringing Zcash to the world through practical adoption. It provides Zcash blockchain integrations, technical support, co-marketing coordination, and grants focused on everyday use by businesses, institutions, and projects.

## Background & History

Zcash Labs was publicly announced on August 6, 2026. It operates as an independent entity focused on commercial integrations, infrastructure services, and adoption-oriented funding. The organization was already supporting projects prior to the formal announcement and continues to expand its work in the post-2026 Zcash ecosystem reorganization (alongside the Zcash Foundation for stewardship and ZODL for wallet/protocol development).

Its first publicly supported project is [zcashtocash](https://zcashto.cash/), a service that facilitates peer-to-peer conversion of Zcash to fiat currency using verified payment apps (Venmo, Revolut, Cash App, Chime, Monzo, and Zelle), available in more than 100 geographies.

## Funding Model

Zcash Labs pioneers a market-driven **coinholder retroactive-grants model**:

- It covers the upfront costs of integrations, new projects, and infrastructure itself.
- It pays supported teams directly and funds the work before seeking reimbursement.
- During each retroactive-grants period, it seeks repayment from coinholders **plus a 20% markup** to cover financial risk and operating costs.
- Coinholders decide whether to reimburse each request. If judgment is poor, Zcash Labs bears the loss.

This reduces funding uncertainty for projects and aligns incentives with ZEC holders. Initial requests are intentionally small to test community support for the model.

## How Zcash Labs Helps

Zcash Labs works with projects of every scale through four main activities:

1. **Consulting & advice**  
   Helps businesses assess whether Zcash is the right tool and advises on practical use in operations.

2. **Technical support & solutions**  
   Provides hands-on support and builds custom solutions for institutions already integrating or needing technical work around Zcash.

3. **Grants & co-marketing**  
   Directly funds promising projects and coordinates co-marketing to reach the right audiences.

4. **Coinholder retroactive grants**  
   Fronts costs for valuable work and seeks reimbursement through the coinholder process.

### Process (from first conversation to something running)

1. Understand the fit — honest assessment of whether Zcash solves the problem.
2. Advise on the approach — map flows, trade-offs, and requirements.
3. Build & support — technical implementation and ongoing help.
4. Fund what’s promising — grants and co-marketing where they expand real Zcash use.

## Infrastructure

Zcash Labs operates independent, privacy-preserving Zcash infrastructure, including:

- A Shielded Vote Validator
- lightwalletd instances
- Full-node instances
- Plans for additional RPC services

These provide users and integrators with independent options for accessing the network.

## Role in the Zcash Ecosystem

Zcash Labs focuses on the **commercial integrations, infrastructure services, and adoption financing** layer of the stack. It complements:

- Zcash Foundation (stewardship, core infrastructure, governance)
- ZODL (wallet and protocol development)
- Other independent teams (Shielded Labs, Obscura Labs, Zingo Labs, etc.)

By fronting costs and seeking coinholder reimbursement, it tests and expands a market-driven path for funding practical Zcash use.
